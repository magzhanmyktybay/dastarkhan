Why guests come back

We cook the dishes Kazakh families make for guests, without shortcuts.

Recipes from home

Our head chef learned to cook from her grandmother in Shymkent. The noodle dough for beshbarmak is still rolled by hand every morning.

Meat from the steppe

Lamb and horse meat come from two family farms in Akmola region and are delivered three times a week.

Room for everyone

Tables for two, a family hall for up to twenty guests and private rooms decorated like a yurt for celebrations.
