Our story

A small family kitchen that grew into one of Astana's favourite places for a proper Kazakh meal.

A family table since 2012

A dastarkhan is the Kazakh table set for guests: a cloth covered with bread, sweets, meat and endless cups of tea. When the Nurlanov family opened their first room with eight tables, they wanted every guest to feel that welcome.

Today we have three halls, but the idea is the same. We cook the dishes we grew up with, we use local meat and dairy, and we never rush anyone out of their seat.

How we grew

From one room to three halls, in four steps.

  1. 2012

    The first Dastarkhan opens with eight tables and one cook.

  2. 2016

    We open the family hall for weddings, birthdays and toi celebrations.

  3. 2020

    Delivery starts across Astana, with beshbarmak packed to stay warm.

  4. 2024

    Two private yurt-style rooms are added for smaller groups.

What we promise
